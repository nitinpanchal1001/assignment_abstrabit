# Northwind Logistics — Operations Handbook

Revision 7.2 · Effective 1 March 2026 · Owner: Operations Standards Group

## 1. Purpose and scope

This handbook governs day-to-day freight operations across all Northwind Logistics
depots in the United Kingdom and the Republic of Ireland. It applies to employed
drivers, agency drivers, depot coordinators and third-party carriers operating
under a Northwind contract.

It does not cover warehouse automation, which is documented separately in the
Mechanical Handling Standard.

## 2. Emergency freight surcharge

When a consignment is accepted inside the emergency window — less than six hours
before the requested collection time — an **emergency freight surcharge of 14.5%**
is applied to the base rate.

The surcharge is calculated on the base rate only. It is never applied to fuel
levies, tolls, or waiting time, and it cannot be combined with the volume
discount described in section 5.

Depot coordinators may waive the surcharge once per customer per quarter. Any
further waiver requires written approval from a Regional Operations Manager.

## 3. Project FALCON-7

**Project FALCON-7** is the internal codename for the Northwind night-freight
corridor between Manchester and Rotterdam, launched in January 2026.

FALCON-7 consignments are subject to three additional rules:

- Trailer seals must be photographed at both origin and destination, and the
  photographs attached to the consignment record within 30 minutes of sealing.
- The corridor operates on a strict 22:00 cut-off. Loads presented after the
  cut-off roll to the following night and are re-rated at standard daytime
  tariff.
- Only drivers holding a current ADR certificate may be rostered onto FALCON-7,
  regardless of whether the specific load contains dangerous goods.

The FALCON-7 service level target is 97.5% on-time arrival, measured monthly
against the Rotterdam gate-in timestamp.

## 4. Vehicle checks

Drivers must complete a walk-around check before first use of a vehicle each day
and record defects in the driver app before departure. A vehicle with a
category-A defect must not leave the depot under any circumstances.

Category-A defects include: brake system warnings, tyre damage exposing cords,
steering play beyond tolerance, and any load-securing equipment failure.

## 5. Volume discount

Customers shipping more than 400 pallets in a calendar month qualify for a 6%
volume discount on the following month's base rates. The discount is applied
automatically by the rating engine and does not need to be requested.

The volume discount and the emergency freight surcharge are mutually exclusive.
Where both would apply to the same consignment, neither is applied and the
consignment is rated at standard tariff.

## 6. Waiting time

The first 45 minutes of waiting time at collection or delivery is free. Beyond
that, waiting time is charged in 15-minute increments at the rate published in
the customer's rate card.

Drivers must record arrival and departure times in the driver app. Waiting time
that is not recorded in the app cannot be invoiced.

## 7. Escalation

Operational incidents are escalated on the following ladder:

1. Depot Coordinator — first response, within 15 minutes.
2. Regional Operations Manager — if unresolved after 1 hour, or immediately for
   any incident involving injury, a category-A defect, or a FALCON-7 load.
3. Director of Operations — if unresolved after 4 hours, or for any incident
   with media or regulatory exposure.
