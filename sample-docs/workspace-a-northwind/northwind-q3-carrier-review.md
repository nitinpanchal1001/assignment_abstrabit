# Northwind Logistics — Q3 2026 Carrier Performance Review

Prepared by the Carrier Management Office · Circulated 12 October 2026

## Executive summary

Overall carrier on-time performance for Q3 2026 was **91.3%**, against a target
of 94%. The shortfall is concentrated in two carriers and in the Midlands
region; performance elsewhere was at or above target.

Two carriers are recommended for formal review. One carrier is recommended for
expanded allocation.

## Pelham Road Carriers

Pelham Road's on-time performance fell for the third consecutive quarter. The
dominant failure mode is late collection rather than late delivery, which points
at driver availability rather than routing.

The Carrier Management Office recommends a formal review meeting in November and
a 30% reduction in allocated volume until on-time performance exceeds 92% for
two consecutive months.

## Verity Distribution

Verity's damage rate is within tolerance but its invoice dispute count is the
highest on the panel. Twenty-three disputed invoices in a single quarter
represents roughly 9% of all Verity invoices.

Most disputes concern waiting time billed without corresponding driver app
records, which is not invoiceable under section 6 of the Operations Handbook.

## Bramley Haulage

Bramley was the strongest performer on the panel and absorbed additional volume
in August at short notice without a service impact. The office recommends
increasing Bramley's allocation by 15% in Q4 and adding them to the FALCON-7
corridor roster, subject to ADR certification checks.

## Midlands regional shortfall

Midlands on-time performance was 86.9%, roughly four points below the next
weakest region. Root-cause analysis attributes this primarily to the Brierley
Hill depot relocation in July, which was completed three weeks late.

Performance recovered to 92.4% in September, suggesting the effect was
transitional rather than structural.

## Recommendations

1. Open formal review with Pelham Road Carriers and Verity Distribution.
2. Increase Bramley Haulage allocation by 15% in Q4.
3. Re-baseline Midlands targets in Q4 following the depot relocation.
4. Require driver-app waiting-time records before any waiting-time invoice is
   accepted, with no exceptions.
